Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making X-ELRA usable by everyone.

Overview

X-ELRA aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. We are committed to ensuring that all learners, including those using assistive technologies, can access and benefit from the platform.

This statement was last reviewed in March 2026.

Known limitations

  • Some embedded lesson content (iframes) may not fully meet contrast requirements depending on the source material.
  • Interactive code execution environments within lessons may have limited screen reader support.
  • Third-party survey iframes (consent forms, periodic surveys) are outside our direct control and may have their own accessibility limitations.
  • Chart visualisations in researcher-facing analytics views do not currently provide text alternatives.

Assistive technology tested

  • NVDA (Windows) with Firefox and Chrome
  • VoiceOver (macOS/iOS) with Safari
  • Keyboard-only navigation across all major browsers

Feedback and contact

If you encounter accessibility barriers while using X-ELRA, please contact us at Simon.West@port.ac.uk. We welcome feedback and will work to address issues promptly.

When reporting an accessibility issue, please include:

  • A description of the problem and the page where it occurs.
  • The browser and assistive technology you are using.
  • Any steps needed to reproduce the issue.
