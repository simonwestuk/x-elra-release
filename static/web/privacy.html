Privacy & Transparency

This page explains how the X‑ELRA research platform handles your data and how the recommender makes decisions.

Data handling at a glance

  • Purpose: We record interactions to evaluate and improve recommendation quality, explainability, and overload controls.
  • Identifiers: Learners are referenced by the ID or email you provide when signing in. No additional personal data is collected.
  • Events stored: Consent decisions, item impressions, clicks, completions, preference updates, and sentiment feedback are logged.
  • Retention: Data is stored in secured databases aligned to institutional policy. Retention periods follow the approved ethics protocol.
  • Access: Only the research team supporting X‑ELRA can access raw logs. Aggregated analytics may be shared with stakeholders.
  • Opt-out: You can withdraw consent at any time via the consent banner. Doing so stops data collection and purges recent sentiment entries.

How this system makes decisions

The system routes each learner into a predefined learning pathway.
